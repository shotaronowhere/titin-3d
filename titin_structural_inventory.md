## Key structural landmarks for the model

- **Z-disc anchoring**: `1YA5`, `6DL4` (Z1Z2 / ZIg10 in complex with telethonin/T-cap) — titin N-terminal anchoring geometry, MEASURED.
- **I-band spring domains**: `1TIT`/`1TIU`/`1WAA` (I27/I91, the canonical mechanically-studied Ig), `7AHS` (N2A Ig81-83), `7NIP` (UN2A core) — I-band domain folds and N2A element, MEASURED.
- **A-band tandems**: `2J8H`/`2J8O` (A168-A170), `3LPW` (FnIII tandem A77-A78) — inter-domain angles/geometry of A-band super-repeat, MEASURED.
- **Kinase**: `1TKI` (autoinhibited titin kinase) — A/M junction, MEASURED.
- **M-line anchoring**: `2WP3`/`2Y9R`/`3Q5O` (M10 + obscurin/obsl1), `3PUC` (M7, 0.96 Å) — C-terminal anchoring, MEASURED.
- **Thick-filament context**: `8G4L` (2023 cryo-EM human cardiac myosin filament) — titin in situ within the A-band, MEASURED (near-atomic).

## Coverage & known limitations

- **AlphaFold DB does NOT host full-length titin** (34,350 aa exceeds the per-entry length limit; API returns 404 for Q8WZ42). Domain-level AF models must be generated per-domain if needed, and must carry explicit pLDDT confidence flags — never treated as experimental evidence (per master-plan evidence rules).
- **No single experimental structure spans multiple regions** — the sarcomere-scale arrangement is reconstructed from region fragments + the 2023 filament cryo-EM + fiber diffraction, not from one continuous structure. This is the central "STRONGLY INFERRED vs MEASURED" boundary for the model.
- **PEVK region**: intrinsically disordered; no folded experimental structure exists (correctly UNKNOWN at atomic level; mechanics characterized by single-molecule force spectroscopy).
- **Transverse lattice packing** (radial thick–thin spacing): from X-ray fiber diffraction, not in this PDB set.
