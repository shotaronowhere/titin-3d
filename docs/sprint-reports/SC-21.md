## What was implemented

`data/mechanical_parameters.json` is required by `SpecLoader`, embedded in the standalone build,
included in the quantitative model identity, and passed with the injected model fingerprint to
`MechanicalModel`. It records every force-law input, contour binding, numerical option, source and
locator, species, muscle/preparation, temperature, applicability, transfer rationale, decision
state, regime policy, and sensitivity authority.

`scripts/mechanical_model.py` and `src/geometry/MechanicalModel.js` now contain equations and
numeric algorithms only. Both resolve material inputs from the canonical record and contours from
`data/titin.json`. The browser constructor is exactly the injected boundary:

```js
new MechanicalModel(spec.titin, spec.mechanicalParameters, spec.identity.model_fingerprint)
```

The development solve remains available under explicitly diagnostic method/command names to lay
out deterministic regional geometry and prove implementation parity. The ordinary evaluation,
geometry API, force-curve presenter, generated model, and structural-state provenance do not expose
that scalar as a force prediction.

## Sensitivity and uncertainty

PEVK persistence length and stretch modulus, and N2A persistence length. No reviewer-approved
range or covariance policy exists, so `approved_scenarios` is empty and the generated envelope is
`null` for force, regional extension, and incremental compliance.

The UI labels the absent future construct “parameter sensitivity range” and explicitly distinguishes
it from a confidence interval, biological population variance, and experimental error. It displays
added regional length separately and states that absolute regional length is not a compliance share.
Incremental compliance is shown as `not evaluated — no approved parameter range`.

The approved path is implemented and parity-tested with a non-authoritative fixture: it computes
deterministic min/max force, regional-extension, and incremental-compliance envelopes across named

through geometry, generated exports, release validation, and the public chart. The fixture does not
alter the live decision or supply scientific values. Activation still requires a new specialist-bound

## Validation and destructive controls

`scripts/validate_mechanical_parameters.py` rejects missing units, uncertainty/ranges, source
locators, species/preparation/applicability, transfer rationale, decision mismatches, incomplete
parameter inventories, invalid solver policy, invalid regime ordering, fabricated supported
boundaries, invented sensitivity scenarios, and force leakage under an unapproved decision. Its
approved branch additionally requires a named independent specialist; validated target
applicability; ordered slack, supported, and unfolding boundaries; approved uncertainty/validity
ranges; complete positive scenario overrides; sensitivity-derived precision; and a regime-authorized
output policy. Approval is also byte/field-bound to a structured SD-04 ruling naming the exact
parameter-set ID, target accession, supported/slack/unfolding boundaries, approved scenario IDs,
output authorization, and implementation record. Deferred caveat language is rejected after
approval, and source-bound central values must fall inside both uncertainty and validity ranges.

The scientific-decision validators also accept the required human transition without weakening the
existing AI-provenance checks: an approved SD-04 must carry the specialist's name, affiliation, exact
required role, completed independent-review status, and a matching named implementation reviewer.
AI-only implementation verification remains valid for today's deferred ruling but cannot complete a
future human-approved SD-04.

`scripts/neg_control_mechanical_parameters.py` first proves one coherent approved fixture is accepted,
then proves 19 representative mutations are rejected. The approved mutations cover reversed and
overlapping boundaries, unknown and incomplete scenario overrides, absent specialist authority,
unvalidated target applicability, absent approved uncertainty, and a still-suppressed output policy,
specialist-ruling drift, and a double-counted PEVK residue-rise/contour scenario, in addition to the
deferred-record controls.

Both solvers verify the requested extension is bracketed before bisection and reject unreachable
targets rather than returning a bracket edge that violates conservation. The approved fixture also
proves Python/JavaScript agreement for force, sensitivity bounds, and displayed precision. Its
property suite proves monotone force and regional extension, exact total-length conservation, common
series force, sensitivity containment, and continuity across supported/extrapolated transitions.

The generated `data/mechanical_model.json` uses `titin-mechanical-model/2`. Every state row records
the parameter-set ID and exact quantitative model fingerprint, plus null evaluation, sensitivity,
and incremental-compliance values. `scripts/mechanical_model.py --check` gates it and the generated
