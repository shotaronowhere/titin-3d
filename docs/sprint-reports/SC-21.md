## Literature ruling and corrections

The exact ruling is byte-bound at
`docs/scientific-decisions/SC-21/SD-04-LITERATURE-RULING.md`. It audits primary work from the
Linke, Granzier, Fernández, Gautel/Simmons, Kellermayer, and Herzog groups in PNAS, Journal of Cell
Science, Journal of Cell Biology, Biophysical Journal, Cell Reports, Frontiers in Physiology, and
American Journal of Physiology—Cell Physiology.

The audit corrected a real source mismatch: the prior PEVK contour record used 0.30 nm/residue,
while its cited Linke et al. source used 0.34 nm/residue. The central Q8WZ42-1 PEVK contour proxy is
now 1,807 × 0.34 = 614.38 nm. The alternate 0.38 nm/residue convention is retained as a documented
sensitivity endpoint. The central N2A persistence length is likewise corrected from 0.35 to the
reported no-CARP value of 0.34 nm.

Central parameters and every sensitivity endpoint carry units, exact source locators,
species/tissue/preparation, applicability, transfer rationale, approved range, decision authority,
and target limitations. Nine one-at-a-time scenarios cover tandem-Ig persistence, N2A persistence,
PEVK persistence, PEVK stretch modulus, and PEVK residue rise. Each scenario must cite a registered
source and remain within its approved parameter range or validation fails.

The 2,500 nm cutoff is a conservative project boundary, not a measured biological transition. The
public band is a **one-at-a-time literature parameter sensitivity range**, never a confidence
interval, biological population variance, covariance model, or experimental error bar.

## Implemented mechanics and public surface

The Python and JavaScript ports read one canonical record and target-sequence contours. Their dense
parity grid includes every regime boundary and compares status, force, sensitivity, regional
extension, compliance, and precision at `1e-9` pN/nm tolerances. Both reject unbracketed targets.

The stage and Measure tab consume the status-bearing evaluator. In an evaluated regime they display
a sensitivity-derived value, supported-range shading, dashed extrapolated segments, and the
parameter sensitivity band. The audit exposes equations, all 13 inputs, source preparation,
locator, applicability, transfer, validity, and the honest non-human decision authority. In an
omission regime the numeric plot/readout is suppressed and force remains null.

At the two named supported keyframes, the current central outputs are approximately 0.47 pN at
2,200 nm and 1.28 pN at 2,400 nm. These are model outputs, not measurements; displayed precision is
reduced to `0.5 ± 0.2 pN` and `1.3 ± 0.8 pN`, respectively, from the approved sensitivity envelope.

## Validation

`scripts/validate_mechanical_parameters.py` accepts either complete, honestly recorded human
review or the repository's owner-authorized citation-backed adjudication. It never converts the
latter into a human claim. It also rejects stale deferred wording, incomplete parameter metadata,
uncited/out-of-range scenarios, unordered boundaries, authority mismatches, ruling drift, force
leakage, and double-counted PEVK contour overrides.

`scripts/neg_control_mechanical_parameters.py` rejects 19/19 destructive mutations. The generated
`data/mechanical_model.json` and `data/structural_states.json` are byte-checked, and the exact
parameter record is exported in the release pack.

The separate project-wide human release-review policy remains in force. It is a release gate, not a
claim that SD-04 lacks the scientific review standard the owner authorized here.
