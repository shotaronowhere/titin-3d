# Titin in the sarcomere — scientist-feedback preview

This AI-assisted educational visualization follows one representative titin through a
half-sarcomere, compares its spring and scaffold regions, and lets a reader inspect the
supporting claims, source locators, preparation limits, and model outputs. Independent
scientific validation and human usability review are pending. We are sharing it to solicit
corrections before broader educational use.

**How to open this package.** Extract the complete ZIP and open `index.html` in a desktop
browser with WebGL. Keep the adjacent `release` folder. Nothing here needs a server or a
network connection. If 3D is unavailable, open `release/fallback/scope.svg`, then the other
five SVG slides in that folder — `architecture`, `extension`, `lattice`, `provenance`,
`limitations`; `release/LEARN_TRANSCRIPT.md` supplies the same route as text.
`DELIVERY.md`, beside this note, records the exact candidate identity and verification scope,
including what has **not** been checked.

**Three-minute tour:**

1. Meet the sarcomere: muscle fibers contain repeated Z-disc-to-Z-disc units. Myosin
   supplies ATP-powered motor action; the enlarged view follows titin over one half-unit.
2. Follow one molecule: trace its Z-disc-to-M-line route and distinguish I-band elasticity
   from A-band association with the thick filament.
3. Build and stretch the spring: press Stretch, then Replay stretch. The reset is explicit;
   ordinary navigation preserves length. Watch I-band extension and the modeled passive
   force. The A-band span is fixed in this model.
4. Scaffold the thick filament: examine the association without inferring resolved partner
   coordinates or a settled signaling pathway.
5. What do we know?: distinguish Measured, Modeled, Inferred, Schematic, and Not known
   content using the evidence key.

**Two-minute expert follow-through:** select a visible Titin label, choose “Why we know
this,” then “Sources for this object.” Expand a source record to inspect its exact locator,
species/preparation, and transfer limitation. Return to the stretch beat and select the
force value to open its model/sensitivity explanation. Research also retains molecular
architecture, lattice comparisons, and reproducible exports.

**Scope:** the reference construct is human TTN Q8WZ42-1, a citation-reviewed SC-20
reference sequence carrying **no tissue-specific claim**; its sequence and literature
inputs are explicitly identified. No muscle or tissue is assigned to it, which is a limit
of this model rather than a claim that titin is isoform- or tissue-neutral. The
three-dimensional route is partly schematic and does not depict biological copy number.
N2A includes four Ig domains and a structured UN2A core with flexible flanks. The passive
series-chain model imports parameters across source species and preparations; its pN
values are estimates, not measurements of this complete human construct. Force is reported within the model's
2000–2400 nm supported range, flagged as extrapolated above 2400 and below 2500 nm, and
withheld outside those evaluated regimes. “Supported” denotes the internal applicability
policy. Detailed ± values describe literature-parameter sensitivity, not a confidence
interval. Active cross-bridge cycling, calcium activation, domain unfolding, and a resolved
in-sarcomere signaling mechanism are not simulated.
