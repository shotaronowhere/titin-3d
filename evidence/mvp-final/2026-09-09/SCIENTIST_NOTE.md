## How to interpret the model

The reference is human TTN **Q8WZ42-1**, with **no assigned tissue-specific construct**. That limitation is not a claim that biological titin is independent of tissue or isoform. The three-dimensional route is partly schematic; the displayed representative paths do not establish biological copy number.

Sarcomere length controls passive geometry, not calcium activation or an active-contraction trajectory. Quantitative passive force is an estimate **per titin**, with parameters transferred across identified literature species/preparations. It is not a measured force for this complete human construct or a prediction of whole-muscle force.

The current model evaluates force as supported at 2000–2400 nm, extrapolated above 2400 and below 2500 nm, and withheld outside those regimes. “Supported” refers to the model's declared applicability policy. The detailed ± values and chart band describe literature-parameter sensitivity, **not a confidence interval**. Domain unfolding, active cross-bridge cycling, and a resolved in-sarcomere signaling mechanism are not simulated.

The evidence labels distinguish measured, modeled, inferred, schematic, and unresolved content. Passing software checks does not establish that every biological interpretation is correct. The complete limitations and provenance accompany the visualization in **release/**.
